Privacy Policy

COD Order Confirmation Service · Last updated 20 September 2026

This policy explains how the COD Order Confirmation Service ("the Service") handles information. The Service helps online sellers confirm cash-on-delivery orders with their customers over WhatsApp before a parcel is dispatched.

Who operates the Service

The Service is operated by Muhammad Waqas Khan. For any question about this policy or about data held about you, contact mwaqaskhan921@gmail.com.

What information is processed

  • Your WhatsApp phone number, as provided by WhatsApp when you reply to a message.
  • The content of messages you send to the seller's WhatsApp business number.
  • Order details held by the seller, such as an order reference, items and total.
  • The interpretation of your reply — for example whether it was read as a confirmation, a cancellation or a request to reschedule — together with a confidence score.

Why it is processed

Solely to determine whether an order you placed should be dispatched, delayed, amended or cancelled, and to pass that outcome to the seller and their courier. Your messages are not used for advertising, and are not sold or shared with third parties for marketing.

Automated interpretation of messages

Message text is sent to a large language model provider to classify its intent. The provider processes the text to return a classification and does not use it to train models. Where the system is not confident, the message is shown to a human working for the seller rather than acted on automatically. You can always ask to speak to a person by replying to the conversation.

How long it is kept

Message text and the resulting classification are retained by the seller for as long as needed to fulfil or resolve the order, and to handle disputes about deliveries. Sellers are responsible for deleting records once that purpose ends.

Who it is shared with

  • The seller whose WhatsApp number you messaged.
  • WhatsApp / Meta, as the messaging platform carrying the conversation.
  • The language model provider used to classify message intent.
  • The courier chosen by the seller, for delivery details only.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages about your order.
  • Ask for a human instead of the automated assistant.
  • Request a copy of, correction of, or deletion of the data held about you.

Data deletion

To have your data deleted, email mwaqaskhan921@gmail.com from, or quoting, the phone number used in the conversation. Requests are actioned within 30 days, except where a record must be kept to resolve an ongoing delivery dispute.

Security

Access credentials are kept in environment configuration rather than in source code, and the service endpoint used by the automation is protected by a shared secret. No payment card details are collected or processed by the Service; cash on delivery is settled directly with the courier.

Children

The Service is intended for use by adults placing orders with a seller, and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published at this address with an updated date at the top.
